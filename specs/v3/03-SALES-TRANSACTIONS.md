## Receipts (relates to Req 3.8 and 4.9)

- Printed and shared receipts are black on white only, with no tinted or coloured backgrounds, for thermal-printer and print legibility.
- The brand colour may appear only in an optional on-screen receipt preview header, never in printed or shared output.
